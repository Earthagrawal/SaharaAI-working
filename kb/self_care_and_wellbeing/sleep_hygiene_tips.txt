**Sleep Hygiene Tips**

This document offers practical tips for improving sleep quality.

1.  **Importance of Sleep:** Explain why sleep is crucial for mental and physical health.
    * **Response Snippet:** "Sleep is one of the most powerful tools for your mental health. It helps your brain process emotions, regulate your mood, and recharge."

2.  **Creating a Routine:**
    * **Response Snippet:** "Try to go to bed and wake up at the same time each day, even on weekends. This helps regulate your body's internal clock."

3.  **Wind-Down Period:**
    * **Response Snippet:** "Create a relaxing routine before bed. This could be reading a book (not on a screen), listening to calming music, or taking a warm shower."

4.  **Screen Time:** Advise on limiting screen time before bed.
    * **Response Snippet:** "The blue light from phones and computers can interfere with your sleep. Try to put screens away at least an hour before you plan to sleep."

5.  **Environment:**
    * **Response Snippet:** "Make sure your bedroom is dark, quiet, and cool. A comfortable environment makes it easier to fall and stay asleep."
