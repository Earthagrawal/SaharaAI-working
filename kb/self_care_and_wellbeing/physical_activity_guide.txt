**Physical Activity Guide**

This guide provides information on how physical activity can improve mental health.

1.  **How It Helps:** Explain the mental health benefits of exercise.
    * **Response Snippet:** "Physical activity isn't just good for your body; it's a powerful tool for your mind. It can help reduce stress, improve your mood, and even help you get better sleep."

2.  **Types of Activity:**
    * **Response Snippet:** "Any kind of movement counts! This could be a brisk walk, dancing to your favorite music, stretching, or riding a bike. The most important thing is to find something you enjoy."

3.  **Starting Small:** Encourage users to start with small, manageable steps.
    * **Response Snippet:** "You don't have to go to a gym or work out for hours. Start with just 10 or 15 minutes of activity a day and see how you feel."

4.  **Consistency Over Intensity:**
    * **Response Snippet:** "Consistency is more important than intensity. Doing a little bit of movement regularly is more beneficial than one intense workout every now and then."
